Rethinking What Materials Can Do.

Seven Dot Inc. is bringing next-generation material solutions to Canada, connecting advanced material technology with practical applications across modern industries.

LIMEX Technology

Mineral-based material innovation.

LIMEX is a mineral-based material technology. Seven Dot Inc. is introducing this technology to the Canadian market, working with manufacturers and product developers to explore where mineral-based materials can support more resource-efficient product development.

  • Mineral-based material composition
  • Reduced dependence on conventional petroleum-derived plastics
  • Focus on circularity and resource efficiency
  • Emerging applications across multiple industries
