LIMEX Technology

Mineral-based material innovation.

LIMEX is a material made primarily from limestone (calcium carbonate) — one of the most abundant minerals on Earth — engineered as an alternative to conventional plastic and paper. Seven Dot Inc. is the approved distributor for LIMEX in Canada, supporting manufacturers through material marketing, product trials, and industrial adoption.

  • Made primarily from limestone, reducing reliance on petroleum-derived plastic
  • Can be produced using much of the equipment already found in existing plastics and paper manufacturing lines
  • Multiple grades available depending on application and physical property requirements
  • Currently building relationships with manufacturers across Ontario's plastics-processing industry

Environmental and performance figures vary by product, grade, and application — get in touch for physical property data relevant to your use case.
