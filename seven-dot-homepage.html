What is LIMEX

A material built from limestone, not petroleum.

LIMEX is an advanced material technology, engineered in Japan, using calcium carbonate and other components to create material solutions designed to reduce dependence on conventional petroleum-based plastics. It is supplied in pellet form, engineered for compatibility with established plastic-processing equipment.
